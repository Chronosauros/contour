0:00What Contour does

Contour is an EQ manager for the CrinEar Protocol Micro and Max, FiiO KA15 and K13 R2R, SnowSky Melody and Tiny A, TRN Black Pearl, Moondrop Dawn Pro 2 and Echo-A, and Kiwi Ears Allegro Mini. The dongle keeps its EQ inside, so the EQ works with every app and every device you plug it into.

The film was shot with the Protocol Micro, which has 8 bands; the bands and filters of every DAC are under Limits and troubleshooting below.

Contour changes that EQ from an Android phone, with no account and no internet. A fresh install comes with three profiles to start from: PROFILE 1 (flat), NIGHTFALL and DUSK.

0:26Connecting

Plug in your DAC. A TRN Black Pearl needs your headphones plugged into it first. The pill in the top right corner of the Library shows its status:

  • NO DAC - nothing is plugged in. Tap it to check again.
  • TAP TO CONNECT - the dongle is there, but Android hasn't allowed access yet. Tap it, and allow.
  • CONNECTED, with a green dot - you're ready.

Tip: when Android offers to open Contour, tick "always", and next time it connects by itself.

0:57Sending a profile

Tap a profile to open it on the EQ page. HOLD TO SEND is the only button that writes to the dongle. Hold it until it fills; a tap does nothing. Contour writes every band and the preamp, reads them back, and stores them only if everything matches.

ON DAC means this profile is now in the dongle's memory. Unplug it and use it anywhere. The Library marks it ON DAC too, so you always know what the dongle holds.

FiiO KA15. The KA15 has three user slots. Pick USER1, USER2 or USER3 in the row above HOLD TO SEND; the row shows the name each slot holds. Contour writes the profile to that slot and names the slot after the profile (up to 7 letters and digits). Long-press a slot to rename it without changing its EQ. While it reads or writes, Contour plays a silent stream to the KA15, because the KA15 answers only while audio is streaming to it; your music keeps playing.

FiiO K13 R2R. The K13 R2R has ten user slots. Pick a slot from 1 to 10 in the row above HOLD TO SEND (the chosen one shows USER under its number); Contour writes the profile to that slot and makes it the active one. The slots keep their USER names. The K13 always holds ten bands, so a shorter profile is filled up with flat bands. A send takes about five seconds, because the K13 needs a short pause after each write to keep it after you turn it off. Switch to BYPASS or a stock preset on the K13 itself.

SnowSky Melody and Tiny A. Both have three user slots. Pick USER1, USER2 or USER3 in the row above HOLD TO SEND; Contour writes the profile to that slot, checks it and makes it the active one. They always hold ten bands, so a shorter profile is filled up with flat bands.

Moondrop Dawn Pro 2. It has 8 bands and a custom EQ, the one the Moondrop app (Moondrop Hub) uses; Contour writes the profile to the DAC's active PEQ bank, reads it back, and never writes over one of the DAC's own presets. The custom EQ has to be switched on in the DAC: according to Moondrop it is off after every plug-in, and both volume buttons held for 2 seconds toggle it.

Moondrop Echo-A. It has 8 bands, with PEAK, LOW SHELF, LOW PASS and HIGH PASS filters. A HIGH SHELF is made from a low shelf and the preamp, so its gain goes in whole dB, and Contour may lower the preamp to fit it (for example from -6 to -9 dB).

Kiwi Ears Allegro Mini. It has 5 bands, PEAK only, and no preamp. A profile with more bands, other filter types or a preamp can't be sent until you change it; the red line under HOLD TO SEND says what to change. After each send the Allegro Mini drops off USB for about a second and comes back on its own. The DAC works out its filters as if the stream ran at 24 kHz, so Contour assumes the usual 48 kHz stream and writes the frequencies converted for it.

USER slots. On a DAC with USER slots, tap a slot to switch the DAC to it and see the EQ it holds. If that EQ is already in your Library, Contour opens that profile; if not, it adds it as a new one. After you plug the DAC in, Contour reads every USER slot and goes back to the one that was playing. Nothing on the DAC changes until you hold HOLD TO SEND.

1:29A new profile

To make a new profile, tap the empty card with the plus. FLAT gives you one flat band at 1 kHz.

The white line is what your EQ does to the sound. Each numbered dot is a band: drag it sideways for frequency, up and down for gain. Long-press an empty spot to add a band right there.

1:54Filter types and sliders

Under the graph, pick the filter type: PEAK works around the frequency, LOW SHELF below it, HIGH SHELF above it. Some DACs also have HIGH PASS, which cuts below the frequency, LOW PASS, which cuts above it, and, on FiiO and SnowSky, BAND PASS and ALL PASS. Contour shows only the types your DAC has.

The FREQ, GAIN and Q sliders move from where they are, so a touch never makes the value jump. Slow drags give fine steps, and when you lift your finger, the value stays.

With PREAMP on AUTO, Contour lowers it just enough to stop your boosts from clipping. Everything saves as you go.

2:23Name and icon

Long-press a card to edit its name and description, and to pick an icon. A good habit: one profile per pair of headphones or IEMs, named after them. Then open it and send it with HOLD TO SEND.

2:47Bands up close

EQ and LIBRARY are also a swipe apart.

Tap a dot or a chip to select its band. Double-tap a dot to reset its gain to 0 dB. Pinch to make the selected band narrower or wider: that's its Q.

The + chip adds a band, up to as many as your DAC has (eight on the Protocol Micro in the film). Tap the selected chip once more to BYPASS the band, enable it again, or DELETE it; a bypassed band shows as an outlined chip. The minus chip next to + deletes the selected band. Undo brings it back.

3:16Exact values and preamp

One fast swipe on FREQ covers the whole range. For an exact value, tap the number and type it.

PREAMP switches between AUTO and MANUAL. In MANUAL, drag it or type a value within your DAC's preamp range (see Limits and troubleshooting). A quieter sound after sending is the preamp making room for your boosts.

Every value that goes to the dongle is rounded toward quieter, never louder. If AUTO would need more than your DAC's lowest preamp, or a band can't be stored exactly as you set it (a big treble boost with HIGH SHELF on the Protocol Max), HOLD TO SEND is blocked with a red line that says why. Lower the boost and send again.

3:46Compare, undo, go back

Compare. A/B works while the profile is ON DAC. Tap it, and the curve switches off but the preamp stays, so you hear the difference at the same volume. Tap A/B again, and your EQ is back. A/B never saves anything to the dongle. A/B is for the Protocol Micro only for now; it is not available on the other DACs yet.

Undo. The two arrows by the profile name work like Ctrl+Z on a computer: undo takes back your last change, whatever it was, and redo steps forward again. One touch is one step, however far you drag. Each profile keeps up to 100 steps, even after you close the app.

Clear. CLEAR EQ leaves one flat band. That's a change like any other, so undo brings it straight back.

Saved version. Every profile keeps a SAVED version, and its Library card shows it. The EQ page edits a working copy, so CLEAR EQ never empties the card. HOLD TO SEND saves the profile. Swipe a card right to SAVE what you have now, or RESTORE to bring the last saved version back; a full swipe right saves. The film still shows LAST SENT, the button this replaced.

5:06Organise, share, import

Duplicate is handy before you experiment: long-press a card and tap DUPLICATE.

Swipe a card left to ARCHIVE or DELETE it. Both offer UNDO for five seconds.

More in the Library

Not in the film yet.

Archived profiles wait in a folded section at the bottom of the Library. Open it, and swipe a card to UNARCHIVE it.

SHARE (long-press a card) sends a profile as plain text, in the Equalizer APO format. SAVE .TXT, next to it, saves the same text as a file that squig.link and graph.hangout.audio can import. Keep a copy somewhere: profiles live only on your phone.

PASTE (tap the + card) reads the same format, from AutoEQ, Squiglink or an EQ by Ear export. OPEN FILE, next to it, reads a .txt file, and Contour also shows up in Open with and Share for .txt files. Empty 0 dB filters are skipped. Check the curve before sending.

FROM DAC (tap the + card) saves what the dongle holds right now as a new profile.

Swiping the + card left offers SAVE ALL: every profile is saved as it is now, without sending anything. It asks first, and it can't be undone.

Service screen and About

Not in the film yet.

Long-press the status pill for the service screen. It shows the raw state of the dongle, and a USB log for bug reports. Read from DAC reads it again. Restore flat writes a flat EQ to the dongle, after asking first.

ABOUT & LICENCES, at the bottom of the Library, shows the version and the open-source licences.

DAC page and factory reset

Not in the film yet.

A DAC with its own settings gets a third page, DAC, next to EQ and LIBRARY. It shows only what your DAC has, with the sample rate and the firmware at the top: on the FiiO KA15 VOLUME, VOLUME STEPS, BALANCE, FILTER, OUTPUT, DRE, BRIGHTNESS, SCREEN OFF, ROTATION and LANGUAGE; on the SnowSky Melody VOLUME, VOLUME STEPS, BALANCE, FILTER, GAIN, OUTPUT, DRE, IN-LINE MIC, LIGHT and BUTTONS; on the SnowSky Tiny A VOLUME, VOLUME STEPS, BALANCE, IN-LINE MIC and LIGHT. USB AUDIO (UAC) is shown on these DACs but can't be changed from Contour.

On the Moondrop Dawn Pro 2 the DAC page shows EQ INDEX, GLOBAL GAIN and PRE GAIN, for reading only. On the Kiwi Ears Allegro Mini it has the EQ switch (OFF or ON) and GLOBAL GAIN in steps from -18 to +3 dB; HOLD TO SEND leaves the switch where it is.

FACTORY RESET is at the bottom of the DAC page on the FiiO KA15 and the SnowSky Melody. SAVE A COPY AND RESET first reads your USER slots, their names and the settings and keeps them on the phone, then resets the DAC and shows what changed. WRITE THE SAVED COPY BACK puts your slots and settings back. After the reset, unplug the DAC and plug it back in; on the Melody Contour waits for that with no time limit, and STOP WAITING ends the wait. The Tiny A has no reset button, because its DAC ignores the command.

5:27Limits and troubleshooting

Contour never lets you go past your DAC's limits, so what you see is what it plays. Q goes from 0.1 to 10 and frequency from 20 Hz to 20 kHz on every DAC unless the table says otherwise.

The Micro and the Echo-A have no real high shelf, so Contour sends a mirrored low shelf plus preamp: the same curve. The Max, KA15, K13 R2R, Melody, Tiny A, Black Pearl and Dawn Pro 2 have native shelf filters and need no mirroring. If the KA15 stops answering after a save, unplug it and plug it back in.

  • Stuck on NO DAC? Plug the dongle in again, without adapters or hubs. A TRN Black Pearl shows up only with headphones plugged into it.
  • Sending failed? Hold the button again. Usually the dongle moved.
  • PASTE greyed out? The clipboard holds no EQ that Contour understands. Copy the text again.

Installing, updating and checking the APK are on the install page.

6:05Where to go next

Other dongles may follow, once someone confirms them on real hardware. Contour is open source: the code and the releases are on GitHub, and questions and bug reports go to GitHub Issues.
