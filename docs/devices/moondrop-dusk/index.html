Moondrop DUSK-SP EQ app for Android

Contour EQ is a free, open-source Android app that reads and writes the parametric EQ stored inside the Moondrop DUSK-SP, a USB cable with a built-in DSP that ends in 2-pin IEM plugs. The EQ is stored in the cable itself, so while the cable plays your Custom EQ, it works in every app, with no system equaliser.

What Contour does not do on the DUSK-SP

  • Firmware updates.
  • Making the Custom EQ play on its own after a plug-in. The cable always starts on DUSK-Default and nothing known changes that, so pick CUSTOM EQ on the DAC page after plugging in.
  • Writing over the five presets. They live in the cable's firmware; Contour writes only the Custom EQ.
  • A preamp. The cable has no preamp setting that can be read back, so Contour offers none.
  • LOW PASS and HIGH PASS filters. No code for them is known on this cable.
  • A measured sound check. The cable ends in 2-pin IEM plugs, which the test rig cannot take, so the curve was checked by ear, not measured.
  • The Moondrop FreeDSP, MAY and Echo-B. They speak the same protocol, but only the DUSK-SP has been tested, so Contour recognises only the DUSK-SP.

Contour EQ and FreeDSP-Studio

Contour talks to the DUSK-SP in the protocol of FreeDSP-Studio, an open-source tool (MIT licence) whose author tested it on a DUSK-SP. That author reports that Moondrop's own app sets gains in whole dB. Contour sends the exact gain you set in the filter itself, and the cable reports it back rounded to whole dB. Contour runs on the Android phone the cable is plugged into, keeps your profiles in a library on the phone and needs no internet or account.

Using Contour with the DUSK-SP

  1. Keep the profile to 9 bands of PEAK, LOW SHELF or HIGH SHELF, with no preamp.
  2. Send it with HOLD TO SEND. Contour writes all nine bands to the Custom EQ; the cable stores them and restarts, and is back on USB within about a second. Contour then switches it to the Custom EQ and reads every band back.
  3. Android may ask again for permission to use the cable after each send, because the restart reconnects it on USB. Allow it, and Contour finishes the read-back.
  4. After every plug-in the cable plays DUSK-Default. Tap the button at the foot of the EQ page, which then reads DUSK-DEFAULT, and pick CUSTOM EQ on the DAC page to hear your EQ again.

Questions about the DUSK-SP

Is there an app for the Moondrop DUSK-SP cable on Android?

Yes. Contour EQ reads and writes the DUSK-SP's 9-band EQ from an Android phone and switches the cable between your Custom EQ and its five presets. It is free and open source (Apache 2.0).

Why does my EQ stop playing after I unplug the cable?

It is still stored. The DUSK-SP starts on its DUSK-Default preset after every plug-in, and nothing known changes that. Pick CUSTOM EQ on Contour's DAC page and your EQ plays again.

Why does Contour show whole-dB gains after reading the cable?

The cable reports each band's gain rounded to whole dB, so a band sent at 6.7 dB reads back as 7 dB. The cable's sound comes from the filter itself, and Contour writes the exact gain into it; the reported gain is only a label. How closely the cable plays the drawn curve has not been measured.
