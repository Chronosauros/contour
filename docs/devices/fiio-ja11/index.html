FiiO JadeAudio JA11 EQ app for Android

Contour EQ is a free, open-source Android app that reads and writes the parametric EQ stored inside the FiiO JadeAudio JA11. The EQ stays in the dongle, so it works in every app and on every device you plug it into.

What Contour does not do on the JA11

  • Firmware updates.
  • BAND PASS and ALL PASS filters. The JA11 stores them, but BAND PASS played flat in two checks on the test rig, and ALL PASS could not be shown to do anything, so Contour offers neither.
  • Switching to the JA11's stock presets (Vocal, Classic, Bass) or to Close EQ, which turns the EQ off. Contour could switch to them only without saving, and such a choice is gone after an unplug, so it writes and selects USER1 only.
  • Changing USB AUDIO. The DAC page shows it, but switching it would make the JA11 reconnect, so Contour does not change it.
  • Resetting the DAC. FiiO Control has no factory reset for the JA11 either.

Using Contour with the JA11

  • Send a profile with HOLD TO SEND. Contour selects USER1, writes the bands and the preamp and saves them. The JA11 then drops off USB for about 2 seconds and comes back on its own; Contour reads every band back once it is back.
  • Android may ask again for permission to use the JA11 after each send and after each IN-LINE MIC change, because the DAC reconnects on USB. Allow it, and Contour finishes the read-back.
  • A new JA11 comes set to Close EQ, with its EQ off. The first send switches it to USER1, so the EQ plays from then on.

Contour EQ and FiiO Control

FiiO's own app for the JA11 is FiiO Control. Both offer 5 bands from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, -12 to +12 dB, Q 0.1 to 10, a preamp, VOLUME and the in-line mic switch. FiiO Control offers PEAK, LOW SHELF and HIGH SHELF; Contour adds LOW PASS and HIGH PASS, which the JA11 played as pass filters on a test rig. FiiO Control also switches the JA11's stock presets, which Contour leaves alone. Contour runs on the Android phone the dongle is plugged into, keeps your profiles in a library on the phone and needs no internet or account.

Questions about the JA11

Is there an EQ app for the FiiO JA11 on Android?

Yes. Contour EQ reads and writes the JA11's 5-band EQ and preamp in USER1 from an Android phone, with LOW PASS and HIGH PASS next to FiiO's own filter types, and sets VOLUME and IN-LINE MIC on its DAC page. It is free and open source (Apache 2.0). FiiO's own app for this DAC is FiiO Control.

Can Contour update the JA11's firmware?

No. Contour does not update firmware. It reads and writes the EQ, the preamp, VOLUME and IN-LINE MIC. It was tested on firmware V2.2 (the USB version reads 2.20); other firmware versions have not been tried.

Do I need a driver or root?

No. Android supports USB audio dongles out of the box, and Contour talks to the JA11 over USB after you allow it in Android's permission prompt. No root, no extra driver.
