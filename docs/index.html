Onethumb.

Edit, keep and send EQ profiles to your DAC.

Shape the parametric EQ on your Android phone, keep a profile for every pair of headphones or IEMs and send one to your DAC with a single hold. The DAC stores it, so it works with every app and every device you plug it into.

Tips buy the next DAC for Contour to support.

Quick.

Pick a profile, hold one button, and your DAC sounds right. No computer, no vendor app.

Thumb-sized.

A parametric EQ is usually a table of numbers on a computer. Contour turns it into a curve you drag with one thumb, with big sliders and an undo for every step.

Switch.

Many dongles keep several EQ presets at once. Tap a slot and the dongle switches straight away, with that slot's EQ on screen and ready to edit.

Fits.

Contour knows which DAC you plugged in and shows only what that DAC can do. A DAC with slots gets slot buttons; one with its own settings gets a DAC page; one without them gets neither. Nothing that does nothing.

Free andopen source.

It needs Android 9 or newer with USB OTG. The app has no internet permission, no accounts and no analytics, so nothing leaves the phone. This website counts anonymous visits, without cookies.
