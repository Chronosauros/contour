USER slots drive the editor: tap a slot to switch the DAC to it and see its EQ. After you plug in, Contour reads every USER slot in the background (FiiO KA15 and K13 R2R, SnowSky Melody and Tiny A). USER slot names on the SnowSky Melody and Tiny A (a send names the slot after the profile, long press to rename); the K13 R2R stores no names, so Contour shows which profile you last sent to each slot. HOLD TO SEND shows SAVING TO DAC with progress, then ON DAC · SAFE TO UNPLUG. The READ button is gone: a slot tap shows what the slot holds, FROM DAC copies it into a new profile. Fixed: a SnowSky DAC plugged in while playing a USER slot no longer fails with "Active bank changed during read", and opening Contour with a DAC already plugged in connects without a tap.
