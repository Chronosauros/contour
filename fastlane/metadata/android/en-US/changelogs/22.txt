Adds the FiiO KA15, tested on the developer's own hardware: pick USER1, USER2 or USER3 above HOLD TO SEND, and Contour writes the profile to that slot and names it after the profile (up to 7 letters and digits). Long-press a slot to rename it. While it reads or writes, Contour plays a silent stream to the KA15, which answers only while audio is streaming; your music keeps playing. If the KA15 ever stops answering after a save, unplug it and plug it back in.
