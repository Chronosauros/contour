New: FiiO K13 R2R (USB 2972:0120): read, and write to USER1-USER10 with read-back. Shorter profiles are filled up to the K13's ten bands with flat ones, and writes are paced so the slot keeps the EQ after power-off. On FiiO DACs HOLD TO SEND now says what blocks a send, for example BAND 10: FREQ 0 HZ - EDIT BAND. Changing the slot during the hold cancels the send. A file of only 0 dB filters now imports as one flat band.
