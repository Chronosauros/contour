<p>A parametric equalizer (PEQ) manager for USB DACs: the CrinEar Protocol Micro and Max, FiiO KA15 and K13 R2R, SnowSky Melody and Tiny A, TRN Black Pearl, Moondrop Dawn Pro 2 and Echo-A, and Kiwi Ears Allegro Mini. Keep your EQ profiles on your phone, plug the dongle in, pick one and send it. Plug in, send, unplug - that is the whole routine.</p>
<p>All ten DACs have a parametric EQ built into the DAC itself. Whatever it holds works with every app and every device you plug it into - no system equalizer, no app running in the background. For the Protocol dongles the official tool is a desktop browser page; Contour puts it on your phone.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Your EQ profiles are saved</b> - one profile per pair of earphones (IEMs) or headphones, or per mood.</li>
<li><b>Sending takes seconds</b> - hold HOLD TO SEND; Contour writes the profile to the DAC, reads it back to check every value and stores it in the dongle's memory.</li>
<li><b>Every profile keeps a saved version</b> - HOLD TO SEND saves it; swipe a Library card right to save or restore it, and CLEAR EQ never empties the card.</li>
<li><b>Tune on the go</b> - drag bands on the response graph, fine-tune with sliders, paste or open an AutoEQ, squig.link or Equalizer APO parametric profile (clipboard or .txt file), or read the current EQ off the dongle. Profiles can be shared as Equalizer APO text or saved as .txt files.</li>
<li><b>Filter types where the DAC has them</b> - PEAK, LOW SHELF and HIGH SHELF, plus HIGH PASS and LOW PASS on most DACs and BAND PASS and ALL PASS on FiiO and SnowSky.</li>
<li><b>The DAC's own settings</b> - on the DACs that have them (FiiO KA15, SnowSky Melody and Tiny A, Moondrop Dawn Pro 2, Kiwi Ears Allegro Mini), a DAC page shows volume, balance, filter, output and the rest. FACTORY RESET on the KA15 and Melody saves a copy of your slots and settings first and offers to write it back.</li>
<li><b>Examples to start from</b> - NIGHTFALL and DUSK next to a flat profile.</li>
</ul>
<p>Contour is free and open source (Apache-2.0). It does not process audio, needs no internet, has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. It only talks to the dongle. Requires Android 9+ and USB host (OTG) support.</p>
<p>Every DAC on the list except the Protocol Max is tested on the developer's own hardware; the Protocol Max was tested by a community member on a real device. Bands: the Protocol Micro, Echo-A and Dawn Pro 2 have 8, the Allegro Mini 5, all the others 10. The FiiO KA15, SnowSky Melody and Tiny A have three USER slots, the FiiO K13 R2R ten. Plug your headphones into the TRN Black Pearl first: without them it does not show up on USB.</p>
<p>Unofficial, not affiliated with CrinEar, FiiO, SnowSky, TRN, Moondrop or Kiwi Ears. It writes to the dongle only on an explicit action and only with documented commands; use at your own risk.</p>
