<p>An on-the-go EQ manager for the CrinEar Protocol Micro USB dongle. Keep your EQ profiles on your phone, plug the dongle in, pick one and send it. Plug in, send, unplug - that is the whole routine.</p>
<p>The Protocol Micro has a parametric EQ built into the dongle. Whatever it holds works with every app and every device you plug it into. The official tool is a desktop browser page; Contour puts it on your phone.</p>
<ul>
<li><b>Your EQs are saved</b> - one profile per pair of earphones, or per mood.</li>
<li><b>Sending takes seconds</b> - hold HOLD TO SEND; Contour writes the profile, reads it back to check every value and stores it in the dongle's memory.</li>
<li><b>Tune on the go</b> - drag bands on the graph, fine-tune with sliders, paste an AutoEQ / Equalizer APO profile from the clipboard, or read the current EQ off the dongle.</li>
<li><b>Examples to start from</b> - NIGHTFALL and DUSK next to a flat profile.</li>
</ul>
<p>Contour does not process audio, needs no internet and has no accounts. It only talks to the dongle. Requires Android 9+ and USB host (OTG) support.</p>
<p>Unofficial, not affiliated with CrinEar. It writes to the dongle only on an explicit action and only with documented commands; use at your own risk.</p>
