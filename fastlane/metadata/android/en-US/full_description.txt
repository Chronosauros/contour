<p>The easiest way to manage your dongle.</p>
<p>Pick one of your profiles. Just hold to send. It's done: Contour writes the EQ into your USB DAC and reads it back to check it. The EQ then runs in the DAC itself, so it works in every app - no system equalizer, nothing running in the background.</p>
<p><b>THUMB SIZED CONTROLS</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Drag bands on the response graph, or add one with +.</li>
<li>Fine-tune frequency, gain and Q with sliders.</li>
<li>Leave the preamp on AUTO or set it by hand in MANUAL.</li>
<li>PEAK on every DAC, plus LOW SHELF, HIGH SHELF, HIGH PASS, LOW PASS and more where your DAC has them.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>SAVE AND MANAGE YOUR EQ LIBRARY</b></p>
<ul>
<li>One profile per pair of earphones or headphones, or per mood.</li>
<li>Every profile keeps a saved version: SAVE your changes or RESTORE the saved one.</li>
<li>ARCHIVE the profiles you don't use, DELETE the ones you don't need.</li>
<li>Import an AutoEQ, squig.link or Equalizer APO parametric profile, or read the EQ that is on the DAC now.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>MADE FOR YOUR DONGLE</b></p>
<p>Contour only shows what's possible, so you don't have to wonder. On DACs with USER slots, pick the slot before you send.</p>
<ul>
<li>CrinEar Protocol Micro and Protocol Max</li>
<li>FiiO KA15 and K13 R2R</li>
<li>SnowSky Melody and Tiny A</li>
<li>TRN Black Pearl</li>
<li>Moondrop Dawn Pro 2 and Echo-A</li>
<li>Kiwi Ears Allegro Mini</li>
</ul>
<p><b>CONTROL EVERYTHING FROM ONE APP</b></p>
<p>On DACs that have them, a DAC page shows volume, balance, filter, output and more.</p>
<p><b>PRIVATE BY DESIGN</b></p>
<p>No account, no ads, no analytics. The app has no internet access and talks only to the DAC you plug in. Your profiles are stored on your phone and leave it only when you share or save them yourself.</p>
<p><b>GOOD TO KNOW</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Needs Android 9 or newer and a phone with USB host (OTG) support.</li>
<li>Contour writes to the DAC only when you ask it to.</li>
<li>Moondrop Dawn Pro 2: its custom EQ has to be switched on. According to Moondrop it is off after every plug-in; holding both volume buttons for 2 seconds toggles it.</li>
<li>The TRN Black Pearl shows up on USB only with headphones plugged into it.</li>
</ul>
